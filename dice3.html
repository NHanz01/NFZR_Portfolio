「 EscapePal 」

A VR Training Experience For Escaping A Sinking Car With An Artificial Intelligence Companion.
Developed by Vnnovation, using Unity and Convai.


About Project


EscapePal is designed to enhance water safety preparedness by simulating real-life emergency situations where vehicles become submerged. The system integrates AI guidance, immersive VR environments, and interactive challenges to train users on critical survival steps such as unbuckling seatbelts, retrieving essentials, escaping vehicles, and breaking windows under pressure. With features like an AI Companion, immersive escape simulation, and self-test challenges, EscapePal equips users with the confidence, decision-making skills, and readiness to act effectively in water emergencies—transforming panic into preparedness and ensuring survival.

DICE 3.0 (Digital Innovation & Creativity Exhibition) was hosted by Universiti Sultan Zainal Abidin (UnisSZA) and the Ministry of Higher Education (MOHE) from 1-4 September 2025 with the theme "Water". DICE 3.0 itself is recognized in the Malaysian Book of Records for achieving the “Most Digital Start-Up Projects by Students in an International Programme.” EscapePal was proudly developed within just one month for the DICE 3.0 competition using the Unity Engine. Aside from nominated as the Top 5 finalists under the Interactive Media (AR/VR) category, EscapePal also earned the Gold Medal and the prestigious Rising Star Start-Up Award.
